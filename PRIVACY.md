# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-02

Desktop Browser for iOS (the "app") is a free, open-source iOS web browser
published by Gumroad, Inc.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no crash
reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. We do not
collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

## What stays on your device

The app uses Apple's WKWebView to render web pages, exactly like Safari.
Standard browser data — cookies, cached resources, local storage,
service-worker caches, and browsing history — is managed by WKWebView and
stored locally in the app's sandbox on your device. None of it is sent to
us; none of it leaves your device unless a website you visit does so (the
same as in any web browser).

You can clear this data at any time by deleting and reinstalling the app.

## Network requests

The only network requests the app makes are the page loads you trigger by
navigating to URLs. Those requests go directly from your device to the
website you visited, over HTTPS where supported by the site. We do not
proxy, intercept, log, or inspect them.

## Permissions

- **Photo Library (add only):** requested only when you tap "Save to
  Photos" on a captured screenshot. Used solely to write that image to
  your Photo Library. Not requested otherwise.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, microphone,
camera, calendar, reminders, health data, or any other system resource.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children
under 13.

## Third parties

We do not share data with third parties because we do not collect any
data.
